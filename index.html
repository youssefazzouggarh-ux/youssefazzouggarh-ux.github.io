    Motivation

    I started as a filmmaker who wanted every frame to feel earned. I still think like a cinematographer, but today my timeline also holds generated shots, restored archives and voices recorded without a booth.

    AI made me faster. Story, rhythm and taste still decide the cut. The new tools let me test ten ideas before lunch and deliver the strongest one in 4K by the end of the week.

    As a strategist I start before the camera rolls: who the film is for, where it will run, and what someone should feel in the first three seconds. Then I build the hero film and every cut-down around that answer.
